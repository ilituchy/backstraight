About

Matt Lituchy

Founder

Matt founded BackStraight Capital Advisors after more than thirty years in commercial real estate, most recently as Chief Investment Officer of Jay Paul Company, where he spent twenty-five years building and financing one of the West Coast's most significant development portfolios.

That experience sits on the ownership side of the table — underwriting the deal, structuring the capital, and carrying the risk — which is a materially different vantage point from intermediating it. BackStraight exists to bring that perspective to owners and developers who need capital raised and structured well.

  • Chief Investment Officer, Jay Paul Company — 2000–2025. Acquisitions, dispositions, and all capital and financing activity.
  • SVP & District Manager, KeyCorp Real Estate Capital. Earlier commercial real estate finance roles in San Francisco and New York.
  • NAIOP — Board of Directors, San Diego and Silicon Valley chapters; Chapter President, 2013.
